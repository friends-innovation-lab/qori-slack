Executive Summary

The three documents agree that a claim status page is a public-facing federal service and must meet Section 508 and WCAG 2.2 AA. They say much less about how status should be worded. Plain-language rules apply, but none of the documents define what each claim stage should be called, and the OIG report finds that current labels mirror internal processing steps.

Key Themes

Theme 1 · Status labels follow internal workflow

Confidence — High

Labels such as "Pending decision approval" and "Preparation for notification" describe what staff do, not what Veterans should expect next.

"Veterans reported that the stage names did not tell them whether any action was required on their part."—

Theme 2 · Timelines must be perceivable without color

Confidence — Moderate

The design system pattern conveys stage progress with color and position; guidance requires a text equivalent for each stage and its state.

Knowledge Gaps and Research Implications

  • No evidence on how screen-reader users move through the status timeline.
  • No guidance on whether estimated completion dates may be shown.
